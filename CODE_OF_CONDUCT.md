# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Bunken welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, physical appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback thoughtfully.
- Focus on the issue, the code, and the project goals rather than individuals.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers privately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's available GitHub contact mechanisms. Please include enough context to investigate the issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
